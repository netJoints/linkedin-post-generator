On this laptop

LinkedIn drafts

A long article, a video caption, swipeable cards, and an animated explainer, written on this laptop. You can also paste a script and make only the animated video. Nothing is sent to a cloud model.

Slide deck Why the cards

What to make

Short subjects, separated by commas or new lines. Up to 12. A sentence here is treated as the subject, not as a request.

What the post should do. This is one brief, not a chat, and the app does not search the web. Paste the announcement pages in Links. Those pages are read as sources. Their URLs are listed in the first comment, and the article may cite them. The short post stays free of URLs so LinkedIn does not turn it into a link preview.

Web pages, blog posts, or video links. One per line, up to five. They are read as source material and listed in the first comment. They are not pasted into the caption. A YouTube link includes the transcript when YouTube provides one. Vimeo links use the title only.

Reference file optional
No file chosen

A PDF, Word document, or text file. Its text is used with the links. One file, up to 8 MB. It is not saved.

Paste the script in your own words. The first line is the headline. A shorter line above a longer headline becomes the small label. Each following line becomes a box. A longer line, or a question, also becomes the line at the bottom. You can edit those words before the video is made.

Video plan

This is the explainer that will be built. The headline stays on screen. The first line is the center box. A later line about an attack, or the last line when there are four or more, sits to the side. The other lines sit above and below. The bottom lines change, in order, as the boxes connect. Edit the words, update the preview, then download the video.

One box per line. Three to six lines. At least two words each.

One line per beat, up to four. These are the words at the bottom of the picture.

Cards

            
Download animated video for a portrait diagram that builds on screen: the headline stays, nodes connect one beat at a time, and the line at the bottom changes. Download video holds each card still for about four seconds. Paste the short post as the caption, then the first comment after you publish. From a personal profile, a video, the image zip, the article, and a text post can be boosted. A PDF document post cannot. The cover is the wide image for the article. Each blog is saved under its own name, such as blog-cisco-live-protect-explainer.mp4, so the next blog does not replace it.
