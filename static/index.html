On this laptop

LinkedIn drafts

A long article, a video caption, swipeable cards, and an animated explainer, written on this laptop. You can also paste a script, turn still pictures and short captions into a video, make a motion film from a few lines, or upload pictures and let the local model write the post. Nothing is sent to a cloud model.

What to make

Short subjects, separated by commas or new lines. Up to 12. A sentence here is treated as the subject, not as a request.

What the post should do. This is one brief, not a chat, and the app does not search the web. Paste the announcement pages in Links. Those pages are read as sources. Their URLs are listed in the first comment, and the article may cite them. The short post stays free of URLs so LinkedIn does not turn it into a link preview.

Web pages, blog posts, or video links. One per line, up to five. They are read as source material and listed in the first comment. They are not pasted into the caption. A YouTube link includes the transcript when YouTube provides one. Vimeo links use the title only.

A PDF, Word document, or text file. Its text is used with the links. One file, up to 8 MB. It is not saved.

Each card is about 11 seconds, so six cards make a video of about 66 seconds. Write each card in your own words. Leave a card empty to skip it. Auto picks the layout from the script:

  • Statement: one sentence, shown large.
  • Question: a question. "This or that?" shows both choices.
  • Steps: numbered lines, or lines that start with first, then, finally.
  • Checklist: bullet lines, or lines that start with a verb such as Log or Keep.
  • Compare: a problem, then "That is why", "Instead", or "But". Lines like "Before: …" and "After: …" also work.
  • Diagram: three or more short sentences, connected as boxes.
Pictures

Up to 10 pictures. JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, or SVG. An animated GIF uses its first frame. Drag a row, or use Up and Down, to set the order. Landscape pictures keep a wide frame, and the whole picture stays visible. The model on this laptop reads them and writes the post. The files are not saved.
